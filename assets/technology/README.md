# Technology and social logos

These SVG files are stored locally so the portfolio does not depend on a runtime icon CDN.

- Technology logos are sourced from [Devicon v2.17.0](https://github.com/devicons/devicon/tree/v2.17.0/icons) under the included MIT license.
- `.NET` and the contact-service marks are sourced from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons) under the included CC0 license. The marks remain trademarks of their respective owners.
- `indexeddb.svg` uses Google's generic Material `storage` symbol because IndexedDB does not have a separate official product logo. It is sourced from [Google Material Design Icons](https://github.com/google/material-design-icons) under the included Apache 2.0 license.

Do not redraw these marks in the site CSS. When a source is updated, replace the SVG file and retain its original proportions and colors.
